# BETA-CONV-01A — EVAL-READY-01 Exception-Driven Governance Amendment

**Effective:** 2026-11-06  
**Status:** Human-authorized superseding working policy for subsequent Seneca
development work.

The Human authorized exception-driven governance for work after the prior
task-by-task approval model. Within an already approved milestone, Codex may
autonomously implement, correct, test, document, commit, push to the authorized
branch, and address ordinary independent-review findings.

Human involvement is reserved for:

1. A working product result ready for meaningful direct Human assessment.
2. An unexpected detour from accepted architecture, specifications, scientific
   method, or project objectives.
3. A consequential decision affecting material risk, cost, scientific
   validity, protected data, user-facing behavior, or a difficult-to-reverse
   commitment.

This amendment does not rewrite historical authorities or acceptance records.
Existing restrictions on real-model evaluation, planner activation, and
promotion remain in force until their specific gates are authorized. This
EVAL-READY-01 preparation authority permits no real-model inference, held-out
scoring, activation, or promotion.
