The reranker fix is ready for independent technical review on [corrective/18d-r-reranker-initialization](https://github.com/cschrupp/offline-rag/tree/corrective/18d-r-reranker-initialization). The implementation is `9c92e706c4b9e9d9ac0816a655bdf131cc63fca1`, based on Docling evidence head `155ccf0cff57010fd926655676da9c318208cfac`.

Concurrent requests could enter `from_pretrained()` on the same shared reranker. The fix serializes first use and publishes the tokenizer and model together after successful device transfer and meta-tensor validation. A failed load leaves the instance available for retry. Initialized requests bypass the lock. Transformers’ process-wide initialization state makes overlap a plausible cause of the incident. The traces do not establish that cause, and separate model loaders remain outside this lock.
