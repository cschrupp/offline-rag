# Proposed Human authorizations for later campaigns

These are proposed exact scopes for separate decisions. This revision
grants none of them. A corpus roster, reference host and test lane must
be approved before a request that depends on them.

## E1 — Fixed-depth development qualification

> Authorize an isolated, offline BGE-30 development comparison on the 12
> approved, family-disjoint queries, using the pinned CPU image and models.
> Compare consecutive with sorted-eight at the same scope and candidate
> order. One warm-up per arm and three measured pairs per case, up to 96
> assembler calls, no retries. Retain input and scientific parity, full
> timing, errors and resource observations. Report and, if supported,
> propose one deterministic eligibility predicate. No holdout, generator,
> public traffic, runtime default changes, or deployment.

## E2 — Fixed-depth held-out execution and optional load

> After roster and gate freeze, authorize the one offline 48-query
> BGE-30 consecutive vs sorted-eight run under the frozen protocol,
> up to 384 assembler calls, with no retries. A conditional strategy,
> if frozen, may be evaluated only by a separate approved extension with
> exact call budget, no tuning from held-out outcomes, and the same
> inputs, baseline and parity gates. No generator or rollout. Stop on
> identity drift and report all measurement or parity failures.

> Separately authorize bounded, nonproduction Docker load and lifecycle
> qualification on eight named, approved queries from the execution
> roster or equivalent synthetic fixtures. Use both profiles, at most
> 16 completions or rejected submissions per arm at each offered-client
> setting 1, 2 and 4, for at most 96 submissions. Authorize up to 24
> additional deterministic HTTP fixture submissions for overload, deadline,
> disconnect, shutdown and cited-response regression checks. Freeze exact
> resource and error limits before this run. No external provider or
> mutation of product data. Stop after evidence capture and teardown.

## Q1 — Independent labels and held-out preparation

> Authorize discovery, rights/access clearance, sampling, and assignment
> of the approved human reviewers for a development-only pool of up to 32
> queries and an unopened held-out roster up to 192. For development only,
> authorize two scoped assembler calls per query (up to 64, no retries)
> to prepare blinded passage and context packets and size the held-out
> study. Use the current BGE-30 control and separately validated BGE-15
> snapshots only in an isolated store. No generator inference. Freeze
> case roster, statistical method, query-level harm allowance, exact
> candidate batching policy and scorer using only development evidence.
> Return the freeze candidate for review before held-out execution.

## Q2 — BGE-15 held-out capture and scoring

> After review of exact frozen artifacts and independent adjudication
> assignment, authorize one scoped retrieval-and-context call per arm per
> frozen held-out query, up to 384 calls, no retries. BGE-15 consumes the
> first 15 original RRF candidates and BGE-30 the original full prefix.
> Use validated immutable publications and paired matching execution
> settings. Store anonymized, blinded packets for the assigned reviewers,
> freeze the adjudicated labels, and score once under the accepted
> non-inferiority and harm rules. No generator, tuning, holdout
> regeneration, deployment, migration or promotion. Preserve partial and
> failed cases and stop for independent evaluation review.

A paired grounded-answer comparison would require an additional decision
covering provider/model, payload authority, source rights, exact cases and
call cap. This plan provides no authorization for provider traffic.
